## Visual assessment (not approval)

The P2 model introduces a long banner/lens staff, narrow ivory mask, dark layered robe, blue-gold accents, and fractured base. It remains distinct from the nearby bright procedural Warrior and its city on the actual Babylon board. At 40px and in grayscale, the staff/mask survive better than the finer robe/armor markings; the slender dark figure has markedly less color contrast and tile footprint than the large bright procedural hero. The portrait’s eight rotations show consistent silhouette, but adjacent mountain/tree scenery can further mute its darker values. Record this for **later lineup-wide tuning**, not a revision now.
