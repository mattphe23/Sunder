5. Reddit "is the game a bit too easy? (even on crazy)" (p3roij)
6. TheGamer review snippet; NintendoWorldReport snippet; Steam discussions snippets

## Complaint clusters

### 1. Tribe/faction imbalance (multiplayer meta)
- Cymanti dominance: ~40–70% of tiny/small MP games; "boring to play against"; hexapod/centipede rush.
- Elyrion Polytaur rush luck-based; Vengir swordsman rush "entirely based on luck of spawn".
- Paid/DLC tribes stronger → pay-to-win perception.
- Players repeatedly beg for a "disable tribes" option in multiplayer.

### 2. Late-game is a "disaster" / repetitive
- Tech, cities, units max out by midgame; nothing left to build toward.
- Star income floods; endless unit spam; WW1 trench-warfare stalemates.
- Score mode past turn 30 anticlimactic; late game "dry", repetitive, frustrating.
- Catapults/ranged dominate late game.

### 3. Faction sameness (base game)
- All classic tribes identical except starting tech → "hugely missed opportunity".

### 4. AI quality
- Single-player too easy even on Crazy; "period of struggle then ruthless dominance and boredom".
- Bots never build advanced units (catapults, cannon boats); AI doesn't scale.

### 5. Luck/spawn dependence
- Spawn position and early ruins/luck decide games; rush success luck-based.

### 6. Technical/multiplayer issues (out of scope for single-player web game)
- Server outages, stalling players who never resign, weekly challenge bugs.

### 7. Misc UX
- "Clunky visuals"/engagement (TheGamer); naval early-game mobility imbalance.

## Implications for our game (draft)
- Make factions genuinely asymmetric but balanced (distinct passive + unique unit each, all free).
- Fix late game: escalating tech costs, meaningful top-tier tech, victory conditions that end games decisively (capital capture), turn-30 score end, avoid star flood (income curve), ranged units counterable (low defense, melee dash punishes).
- AI: scale with difficulty settings; AI uses full unit roster incl. ranged; smarter targeting.
- Reduce luck: fair spawn ring with guaranteed resources near capital; symmetric-ish map option.
- Single-player only → no server/stall issues by design.
- Clean readable UI, fast turns.
